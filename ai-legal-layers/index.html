Conditional route: developing or supplying an AI product

Assess provider status, system/model scope and applicable dates. Where applicable, examine risk and quality management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, instructions and oversight, accuracy/cybersecurity, conformity assessment, declaration/marking/registration, monitoring and serious-incident duties. Assess GPAI duties separately if developing or supplying such a model. Not every AI system needs these measures or external certification.

For certain high-risk deployments, assess whether a fundamental-rights impact assessment (FRIA) is required. A GDPR DPIA and an AI Act FRIA have different triggers.

Definitions, dates & evidence

Implementation dates · checked 30 September 2026

2 February 2025: initial prohibited practices and AI-literacy obligations. 2 August 2025: GPAI obligations began, with transitional provisions. 2 August 2026: general application and transparency requirements, subject to exceptions and transitions.

The Commission reports revised high-risk dates of 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for relevant AI in regulated products. KI-MIG entered into force 29 July 2026. Existing privacy, workplace and other laws continue to apply.
