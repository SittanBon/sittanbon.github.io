Proposed extension · design deep dive

Explore the decisions behind the concept.

These expandable phases describe a proposed route, not the production steps of the four-location rollout.

01Input and brief validationCampaign PDF, human context and missing-information loop+
Human action

Upload the campaign PDF and provide the objective, audience, market, channels, mandatory messages and constraints.

Automation

OpenAI extracts the document, structures the brief and returns questions when information is incomplete or conflicting.

Output

A validated, structured brief that the campaign owner has confirmed.

02Trend research and six visual directionsPerplexity signals combined with campaign strategy+
Human action

Define what must remain true to the brand and which markets or social channels matter.

Automation

Perplexity returns dated, sourced social and visual signals. GPT turns the approved brief and relevant signals into six distinct creative directions.

Output

Six idea cards with concept, visual language, tone, audience relevance and rationale.

03Human selection and concept developmentTwo selected ideas become complete creative routes+
Human action

Compare the six directions, select two and explain what to retain, change, remove or combine.

Automation

GPT develops both directions into campaign concepts, visual systems, design materials, content structures and captions.

Output

Two complete concept routes prepared for creative, brand and compliance review.

04Risk review, iteration and final deliveryVisible flags, detailed feedback and final approval+
Human action
  • Resolve claims, privacy, rights and brand risks
  • Give detailed creative feedback
  • Approve the final production pack
Automation

Flag potential risks, revise only the affected elements and assemble the final mood board, design concept, captions and handoff materials.

Output

An approved campaign package with documented human ownership and a clear revision history.

Responsibility model in the extended design

AI accelerates. People decide.

The split below describes ownership in the proposed creative route. Human approval was also part of the delivered rollout.

AI and automation

  • Extract and structure the campaign brief
  • Research and summarise current trend signals
  • Generate six creative directions
  • Develop two complete concepts and captions
  • Flag risks and apply requested revisions
  • Assemble the final campaign package

Human ownership

  • Define the campaign objective and constraints
  • Confirm the brief and approved source material
  • Select two of the six creative directions
  • Direct detailed creative changes
  • Resolve compliance, rights and brand decisions
  • Give final approval before production or publishing
