AI & Product Solutions.

I look for valuable use cases, make the decision rules explicit and test a narrow route before asking people to adopt a new way of working. The examples below show different levels of maturity—not one blanket claim of “AI transformation.”

Controlled pilot · four-location rollout

GenAI campaign workflow with human gates.

I identified the opportunity, built an n8n proof of concept and compared two AI-assisted locations with two using the traditional method. Standard campaign production moved from roughly two weeks to one. Views, comments and likes were roughly similar, without retained detailed figures.

My contribution: discovery, proof of concept, pilot design, rollout and change support.

Tested OCR pilot · broader product concept

Expense evidence a finance team can review.

I interviewed more than 30 SME finance users and tested OCR extraction on more than 500 receipts. The tested confidence routing sends uncertain items to people; even a high-confidence bill above a project-set amount requires approval. The dashboard is a proposed product experience.

My contribution: user discovery, pilot testing, review rules and product concept.
