Turn scattered expense evidence into trusted financial decisions.

01 · Decision workflow

Automation with a visible escape route.

The system does not treat every prediction equally. It extracts evidence, proposes a match and then routes the transaction by confidence and bill value—giving a green route only to clear cases that do not require value-based approval.

Illustrative decision factors

What should a confidence rating consider?

The pilot tested confidence routing. These six factors explain a transparent design approach; their exact weights are not being claimed as pilot settings.

Transaction match

Is there one strong bank transaction candidate rather than several possible matches?

Field agreement

Do amount, date, currency and merchant agree across receipt and bank evidence?

Extraction quality

How reliably were the required fields read from the uploaded evidence?

Evidence completeness

Are the receipt, mandatory fields and supporting information present and readable?

Policy & context fit

Are the project, category, tax treatment and vendor plausible for this expense?

Anomaly checks

Is the item free from duplicate, tampering, outlier and prior-claim signals?

Proposed evidence controls

Additional product checks could flag suspected duplicates, unreadable evidence, critical mismatches or policy exceptions for human handling.

Confidence and value routing

These bands were tested in the pilot. At exactly 75%, a person reviews the item. A bill above ฿20,000 needs human approval regardless of confidence.

<75%Reject / recapture

Evidence is insufficient or the match is unreliable.

75–95%Human review

A finance specialist confirms or corrects the match.

>95%Green + notify

Notify the owner and keep a log for later audit; the value rule still applies.

Final route after value ruleHuman review

A finance specialist confirms or corrects the match.

Try 75%, 96% and a bill above ฿20,000. This illustrates Bon’s stated routing policy; confidence and value thresholds can be configured differently for each project.
