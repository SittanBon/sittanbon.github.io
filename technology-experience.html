Nanotechnology research: materials, sensing and measurement.

My academic foundation was experimental. I worked with surface modification, gold nanoparticles, thin films and fiber-optic sensing. The research entries below are distinct from a commercial product or medical-device claim.

Research project · 2015–2016

Gold-nanoparticle fiber-optic biosensors

Studied gold nanoparticles on optical-fiber cores and the localized surface plasmon resonance response for sensing. A 2018 ECBA conference abstract publicly lists me as a co-author of related fiber-optic biosensor work.

Research project · 2014 onward

PMMA surface modification

Explored surface techniques for protein binding and fluorescence tracking with gold nanoparticles. This is a research-project description, not a claim of a formal publication.

Research project · 2014 onward

Thin-film preparation and AFM characterization

Prepared PMMA thin films using varied solvents and studied their surface properties with atomic force microscopy.

Research project · 2015

ABO-antigen sensor exploration

Explored antibody and gold-nanoparticle approaches for identifying ABO blood groups from saliva samples. It remains an academic research project, not a clinical product.

Public project descriptions and a co-author abstract are available, but the original lab artifacts and full publication list have not yet been assembled for this portfolio.

Small prototypes, hands-on systems thinking.

These early builds taught me to connect a sensor input with a physical response, then test the behaviour against a simple objective.

Microcontroller prototype

Sun-tracking solar cells

A movable solar-cell setup adjusted its angle as sunlight changed through the day, exploring how positioning could improve capture.

Robotics prototype

Line-following vehicle

A moving car followed a marked line using sensor input and control logic. No performance or commercial outcome is claimed.
